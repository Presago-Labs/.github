# Presago Labs

**Prediction markets on Stellar — positions recorded, resolved, and settled on-chain.**

A poll records what people think. Presago records a position against a defined outcome and settles it under published rules.

## What we build

- **Positions, not opinions** — Participants stake on YES or NO and the contract credits the chosen side.
- **Soroban accounting** — Pool balances, fees, referrals, and payouts are enforced by the contract, not a spreadsheet.
- **Inspectable by default** — Market questions, criteria, resolver authority, and settlement math are all reviewable on the public ledger.
