## Stellar-native by design

This project belongs to the **Stellar** ecosystem — value moves on-chain, and the public ledger means anyone can audit it.

## What we do

A poll records what people think. Presago records a position against a defined outcome and settles it under published rules.

1. **Positions, not opinions** — Participants stake on YES or NO and the contract credits the chosen side.
2. **Soroban accounting** — Pool balances, fees, referrals, and payouts are enforced by the contract, not a spreadsheet.
3. **Inspectable by default** — Market questions, criteria, resolver authority, and settlement math are all reviewable on the public ledger.
